Sources & coverage

What is included?

Selected faculty from the School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCOPE) and School of Computer Science Engineering and Information Systems (SCORE), VIT Vellore. This is a curated collection, not a complete institutional roster. Selection checked whether profile data could be extracted and external author identities could be matched; it did not verify when every VIT page was last updated.

How the records are collected

  • Official VIT pages provide names, designations, departments, qualifications, research interests and outbound research-profile links.
  • Publication providers provide work metadata after an author identity has been checked. A link on a faculty page is a source claim, not sufficient proof of an author match. The accepted OpenAlex author profile is matched by ORCID, compatible name and VIT affiliation; this does not verify every publication individually.
  • Faculty profile contributions provide reported project and patent observations. Unknown dates and roles remain unknown.
  • Monthly research circulars record what VIT announced in that reporting month. They are a historical source archive and have not been imported into this collection yet; they are not a complete record of everyone’s work.

How matching works

Research interests are matched through shared database terms. Publication keywords match the collected text. Department and school filters narrow the results.

One coauthor step means OpenAlex lists both matched author profiles on a paper. Two steps means a recorded mutual author profile connects them; the interface shows that person and both papers. Individual paper records have not been manually reviewed.

No direct or two-step path in this database does not establish that the faculty have never collaborated.

Reading the numbers

Missing metrics are unavailable, never zero by default. h-index, i10-index and citations retain their provider and retrieval date. Retrieval is when we checked the source, not necessarily when it calculated a metric. VIT, OpenAlex and Google Scholar can report different values because they index and count different records. Google Scholar is an outbound link; its profile and metrics are not copied into this site.

Research metrics do not determine the collaborator matches. Journal impact factors are outside this version.
